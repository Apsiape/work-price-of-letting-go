\subsection{Operational access and prior work}\label{hp:sec:access}
\begin{proposition}[\hpproved: marginals do not specify a reset price]\label{hp:prop:W}
The pure state $\hpket W=(\hpket{100}+\hpket{010}+\hpket{001})/\sqrt3$ is uniquely determined among all states by its $AB$ and $BC$ marginals. Nevertheless erasing $A$ using $AB$ while preserving the full $BC$ state, followed by erasure of $BC$, has excess work over joint erasure
\[
 I(A:C|B)_W=h_2(1/3)>0.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Both two-qubit marginals are $\frac13\hpket{00}\hpbra{00}+\frac23\hpket{\psi^+}\hpbra{\psi^+}$. Positivity forces every compatible state into the intersection of their lifted supports. Direct coefficient comparison gives $\operatorname{span}\{\hpket{000},\hpket W\}$. If $a=\hpbra W\sigma\hpket W$, its $AB$ weight on $\psi^+$ is $2a/3$. Thus $a=1$, and positivity and trace one force $\sigma=\hpket W\hpbra W$.

The first reset costs at least $S(AB)-S(B)=S(A|B)$ by the marginal $AB$ entropy balance. Conditional erasure attains it while preserving $BC$~\cite[Corollary 2 and Appendix A]{delRio11}; this preservation includes the reference $C$. The second reset costs $S(BC)$. Subtracting the joint cost $S(ABC)$ gives $I(A:C|B)$. All one-qubit and complementary two-qubit entropies of $W$ are $h_2(1/3)$. Hence the excess is as claimed.
\end{proof}

In particular, the maximum compatible global entropy minus the actual entropy is zero here, yet the schedule surcharge is positive. Inference from overlapping marginals cannot replace the access and preservation rules of a physical operation. The uniqueness phenomenon itself belongs to the marginal-reconstruction literature~\cite{HPlpw}; the example here applies it to a reset deadline. Related operational roles of lost correlations appear in the thermodynamics of modular processing~\cite{BMC18} and in deconstruction and conditional erasure~\cite{HPconditionaldecoupling,HPdeconstruction}. The latter identifies conditional mutual information with the optimal rate of noise that decouples a system while negligibly disturbing a specified marginal; the reset schedule here is a different task.

\paragraph{What is already known.}
Restricted joint access has long had a thermodynamic price. Oppenheim and the Horodeckis introduced work deficit; its pure-state value is the entanglement entropy~\cite{HPdeficit}. This is the sufficiently classical-assisted regime here. Conditional erasure, including negative work, is due to del Rio and collaborators~\cite{delRio11}. The elementary $2:1:0$ comparison is an illustration of that theory. Entropy-production identities are due to Esposito, Lindenberg and Van den Broeck and to Reeb and Wolf~\cite{EspositoLindenbergVandenBroeck10,ReebWolf14}. None of these ingredients is claimed as new.

Devetak's local purity distillation~\cite{HPdevetak} and Krovi--Devetak's bounded classical communication~\cite{HPkrovi} already study distributed purity and communication tradeoffs. Horodecki and collaborators bound the work deficit and the cost of erasing entanglement below by $\hpER$, using the potential on which Lemma~\ref{hp:lem:potential} is built~\cite{HPlocalnonlocal}. The lemma accounts explicitly for local Gibbs baths and quantum transfer; the contribution claimed here is its application to the game-level recovery bounds. Groisman, Popescu and Winter identify mutual information with the minimum asymptotic rate of local noise needed to erase all correlations of a bipartite state~\cite{HPgpw}. These are correlation-cost tasks; the present task also includes preparation, answer release and cyclic reset of a device that must produce a successful game strategy. Our pure-state recovery construction sits in this framework; a communication-work framework by itself is not a novelty claim.

Alimuddin and collaborators study helper-assisted erasure and exclusive control, including Bell certification~\cite{HPalimuddin}. Their target is a specified memory and competing helpers. Here the target includes preparation and cyclic reset of the entire game apparatus, minimized over successful strategies. The required apparatus correlations can diverge with fixed answer alphabets. The distinction concerns the thermodynamic boundary, rather than a new principle of assisted erasure.

\'Cwikli\'nski values supplied XOR side information in a Szilard engine~\cite{HPszilard}, and routes a supplied excitation into a battery on game success~\cite{HPbattery}. Earlier, Maruyama, Morikoshi and Vedral used work extraction to detect entanglement~\cite{HPthermodetection}. These are downstream valuations of supplied correlations or behavior with explicit trusted transducers. They do not establish the upstream minimum cyclic work over game-producing devices studied here. Broader proposals connecting Bell behavior and Landauer cost include~\cite{HPtodd}; no equivalence with the present cyclic task is inferred from a shared vocabulary.

\paragraph{What these appendices add.}
Relative to those sources, the claims advanced here are the game-optimized correlation sandwich, its divergent sharp coefficient for a fixed finite game, the game-level recovery bounds, the paired precision-dependent gap, and the exact four-unit cyclic magic-square construction. The exact-matching sector theorem combines the known trace structure with original-state resource comparisons. The multiphase example in Appendix~\ref{hp:app:multi} supplies a further calibrated price. These are mathematical contribution claims under the stated contracts.

\paragraph{\textnormal{\textsc{Conjecture}}: stability preserving all resources.}
A useful extension would turn approximately matched mixed strategies into sector allocations with quantitative control of loss, the original state's one-way distillable entanglement, and the schedule's work. No such unrestricted equality is proved here. Appendix~\ref{hp:app:rounding} proves an entropy sandwich with a precision gap and rules out fixing the same controls by fiat. Almost-synchronous rounding~\cite{HPvidick} alone does not supply the original-state entanglement control this extension needs.
